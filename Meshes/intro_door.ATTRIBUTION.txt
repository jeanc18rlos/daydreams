The intro level's door is "Classic Interior Door" (Classic_Interior_Door.glb), supplied by the
project owner and originally distributed via Sketchfab.

The GLB is used AS-IS at runtime -- Meshes/Classic_Interior_Door.glb, loaded by
src/ext/gltf_model.rs. Nothing is baked or converted: the model's own smooth normals, tangents,
base colour, normal, occlusion and metallic-roughness maps are what you see. The loader only
fits the model to the engine's scale and resizes the maps to 1024 square (the sources are
4096 square, which is ~400 MB of decoded RGBA for a door a few hundred pixels tall on screen).
